We have Built a membership system for Gym's because right now, The Staff at the front desk of the gym/fitness club is doing most of the work by hand. The gym sells 4 types of plans. After joining the member pays for 1 month and gets a 7-day free trial, after payment is completed, the member becomes active after the 7-day trial if not, it becomes lapsed. The membership can also be paused for 1-3 months (once a year), they don't pay when membership is frozen. If payment is failed 2 times in a row, membership is lapsed and member can't use the gym facilities or book classes until he/she pays what's owed. If membership stays lapsed for 60 days, it is cancelled. Also, different plans has some rules like annual membership fees is paid at once, comes with no free trail and cost €50 to cancel in the first 6 months; student plan need a student ID and off-peak plan only allows weekdays timings between 10:00-16:00.

There are some more rules which classes and billing introduces. The capacity of classes is fixed, after getting full member gets an entry on first come first serve waitlist basis incase if someone cancels, if they miss 3 classes in 30 days, they will not be able to book for week. Each month every member bill is worked out from their plan prices, so the bill is the plan price minus the best discount for the category [corporate (15%), family (10%) or loyalty (5%)], which don't stack. Apart from this any penalties and charges for premium classes are also added and €20 is taken out for a friend referral only up to €60 a year. Because of all these rules system calculates every answer from their membership plan, status, history & date. So, this is a proper backend working system not just a data showing from data base to help front desk do the handy tasks. We've built backend for four main areas membership, booking, membership check-in & billing. It's used through REST API with postman or swagger UI instead of a real front end and json files instead of real databse.
