## Architecture and Design Patterns

- Cockburn, A. (2005) *The Hexagonal (Ports & Adapters) Architecture*, HaT Technical Report 2005.02. Available at: https://alistair.cockburn.us/hexagonal-architecture (Accessed: 7 October 2026).
- Schmidt, D.C., Stal, M., Rohnert, H. and Buschmann, F. (2000) *Pattern-Oriented Software Architecture, Volume 2: Patterns for Concurrent and Networked Objects*. Chichester: Wiley.
- Gamma, E., Helm, R., Johnson, R. and Vlissides, J. (1994) *Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software*. Reading, MA: Addison-Wesley.
- Bass, L., Clements, P. and Kazman, R. (2019) *Software Architecture in Practice*. Boston: Addison-Wesley.
- Fowler, M. (2019) *Refactoring: Improving the Design of Existing Code*. 2nd edn. Boston: Addison-Wesley.
